# Company HR Policy

## Attendance
- Office starts at 09:00. Grace time is 15 minutes. A check-in after 09:15 is **late**.
- A day with less than 5 hours worked is a **half day**. The weekend is Saturday and Sunday.
- One attendance record per employee per day. A second check-in is rejected. The server stamps the time.
- **Absent** means no record and no approved leave. **Missing checkout** means a check-in with no check-out.

## Leave
- Leave quotas per year: annual 14 days, sick 8 days, casual 10 days, unpaid unlimited.
- Only working days count. Weekends and holidays are skipped.
- Overlapping leave or going over the balance is rejected.
- Leave hints: wedding or family event = casual; illness = sick; vacation = annual.

## Payroll
- Gross = 1.5 x basic salary. HRA is 40% and medical is 10% of basic.
- Per-day pay = gross divided by the month's working days.
- Deductions: absent days, unpaid leave days, and half of each half day.
- Joiners and leavers are prorated.
- Tax is calculated on gross x 12 (annual): 0 up to 600k; 5% up to 1.2M;
  30,000 + 15% up to 2.4M; 210,000 + 25% up to 3.6M; 510,000 + 35% above 3.6M.
  (Illustrative.)
- Admin finalizes payroll. A finalized run is locked and can never change.

## Roles
- Admin manages the company.
- HR manages people operations.
- Employees see only their own data.
